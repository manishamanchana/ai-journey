# Social Templates

Don't write these from scratch — pull straight from your project writeup. 10 minutes, two posts.

---

## 💼 LinkedIn post

> **Hook (line 1 — make them stop scrolling):**
> _e.g._ "I just built my first AI [thing] — and one part completely broke my brain. 🧠"
>
> **The build (2–3 lines):**
> What you made and what it does, in plain language.
>
> **The honest bit (2–3 lines):**
> The thing that surprised you, or where you got stuck and how you solved it. This is what makes the post human.
>
> **What's next (1 line):**
> Where you're headed next in the journey.
>
> **CTA + link:**
> "Full writeup 👉 [blog link]" and tag #BuildInPublic #AI #LearningInPublic
>
> 📎 Attach a screenshot or a short screen recording.

**Why this works:** specifics + a real struggle beat polished summaries every time. Post consistently (even weekly) and the algorithm rewards it.

---

## 📸 Instagram carousel (make in Canva)

A 5–6 slide carousel, built from the same writeup:

1. **Title slide** — big, punchy. _"I built an AI [thing] in a weekend"_
2. **The goal** — what you set out to do, 1 sentence + visual
3. **The build** — a screenshot + a line on how it works
4. **The lesson** — the one thing you learned (quotable)
5. **The struggle** — keep it real, what was hard
6. **CTA slide** — "Following my AI journey → @yourhandle" + blog link in bio

**Caption:** reuse your LinkedIn text, add more hashtags (#AIlearningJourney #TechTok #CodeNewbie #100DaysOfCode etc.), and end with a question to drive comments.

---

### ♻️ The repurposing rule
Write the project README once → it becomes the blog post → trim for LinkedIn → visualize for Instagram. **One project, four artifacts.**
